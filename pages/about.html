About us

A new kind of coaching centre, built by students.

EduX was founded by three university students in Bangladesh who believe students learn best when they understand a subject, not when they simply memorize answers.

What we aim for

Concept-focused, student-centred learning

EduX aims to provide a modern learning experience that helps students understand the idea behind each topic, practise it with real problems, and grow confident enough to solve questions on their own. We are starting with Classes 9 and 10 and a focus on SSC preparation, in Bahubal, Habiganj.

Our learning philosophy

Learn to Understand. Prepare to Excel.
